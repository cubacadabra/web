About cubacadabra

A better kind of online world.

cubacadabra is an open-source project in progress. Our long-term goal is to build a meaningfully better Roblox-style platform for creators, children, and parents.

! This is a future plan, not a finished product. None of the ideas on this page are done yet.

The long view

More than “Roblox, but safer.”

Safety is one part of the opportunity. We want to stack better creator economics, modern tools, higher technical ceilings, open formats, and cleaner social design into one more opinionated platform.

For creators

Your work should belong to you.

We’re aiming for clearer economics, easier cash-out, Git-friendly workflows, reproducible builds, documented APIs, portable worlds, and creator identity that feels like participation—not tenancy.

For families

Fun for kids. Easier to trust.

Future safety work may include tighter age separation, no unrestricted adult-to-child contact, transparent moderation, strong reporting, real appeals, and multiplayer where stranger contact is not the default.

Open source, in public

Build the platform in the open.

We plan to open-source most of cubacadabra so developers can inspect, contribute to, and build on the engine, clients, tools, and formats. The production systems that protect people—such as abuse detection, fraud controls, and private infrastructure—may stay private where secrecy materially improves safety.
