Support cubacadabra

A simple monthly subscription lets parents support the work directly, without putting the cost of play on kids.

Open parent-cadabra in My Cube →

cubacadabra is being built as a place for children to play, parents to understand, and creators to build. parent-cadabra is the simplest version of that relationship: parents who want to help fund the platform can contribute $9.99 USD a month directly.

That support keeps the incentives straightforward. Kids do not need to buy casino coins to participate, and the platform can grow around a relationship with families rather than extracting from play.

The subscription is available in My Cube↗ after you sign in. You can review the plan, see your current status, and continue to secure checkout there.
