Cubacadabra A tiny world, in progress

Privacy

We keep the details small.

Cubacadabra is designed to collect only the information needed to run a small, shared online world and keep it a pleasant place to be.

What we handle

Your player, in the moment.

When you join, the service handles your chosen player name, connection information, and in-world activity so it can show you the world and other players can share it with you.

Why we use it

To make the world work.

We use this information to operate the game, maintain the connection, understand what needs fixing, and protect the service from misuse.

What others can see

Shared means visible.

Your player name and presence are visible to people in the same world. Don’t use a name that contains information you wouldn’t want to share with other players.

Your choices

Leave when you’re ready.

You can stop using Cubacadabra at any time. Because the project is still evolving, this page may evolve too; we’ll keep changes here in plain language.
