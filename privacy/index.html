Privacy

How we handle information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information cubacadabra collects, how we use it, when we share it, and the choices available to you when you use our website, game, mobile app, and related services (together, the “Service”).

Effective date: September 3, 2026

1. Information we collect

Only what helps us run the world.

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect or receive the following categories of information:

  • Player information. A player identifier generated for your device or session, your chosen player name, and preferences such as whether you appear hidden in a shared world.
  • Gameplay and connection information. The world you join, connection and disconnection times, player presence, movement or other gameplay events needed to make multiplayer features work, and technical messages exchanged with the Service.
  • Device and network information. Information that your browser, device, operating system, app, or network may provide to our hosting and networking providers, such as IP address, approximate region inferred from an IP address, browser or device type, app version, timestamps, and security or diagnostic information.
  • Information you send us. Information you choose to include when you contact us, such as your email address, message, screenshots, or other details needed to investigate a request or safety report.

2. Information stored on your device

Your name and player ID may stay local.

The website may use browser local storage to remember a generated player identifier and the player name you choose. The mobile app may use the operating system’s local storage for the same purpose. This information remains on your device until you clear the relevant storage, uninstall the app, or change it through an available setting. Clearing it may create a new player identity when you return.

3. What we do not intentionally collect

No account profile or advertising dossier.

The Service does not currently require a traditional account and does not ask for your real name, password, phone number, payment information, contacts, precise location, photos, camera access, microphone recordings, or health information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Based on the current Service, we do not use advertising networks, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-service tracking to build an advertising profile. If these practices change, we will update this Policy and disclose the change as required by law.

4. How we use information

To provide, protect, and improve the Service.

We use information for the following business and operational purposes:

  • to create and maintain a multiplayer connection and display players in the same world;
  • to remember your player name and provide continuity between sessions;
  • to operate, maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the Service and its performance;
  • to detect abuse, cheating, fraud, security incidents, and violations of our Terms;
  • to respond to support requests, safety reports, and legal inquiries; and
  • to comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

5. Legal bases

We use information for recognized legal reasons.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to perform our agreement with you and provide the Service; to pursue our legitimate interests in operating, securing, improving, and protecting the Service; to comply with legal obligations; and, where required, with your consent. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, but withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal or processing based on another lawful basis.

6. What other players can see

Shared means visible.

Your chosen player name, player presence, player identifier or other gameplay state may be transmitted to and visible to people in the same world. Do not choose a name or submit information that reveals something you would not want other players to see. We do not intend for your IP address, device details, or support communications to be displayed to other players.

7. When we share information

Limited sharing keeps the world running.

We may share information with the following recipients and for the following reasons:

  • Infrastructure providers. We use service providers, including hosting, database, networking, security, and content-delivery providers, to operate the Service. These providers may process information on our behalf and are required to protect it and use it only for the services they provide.
  • Other players. As described above, player names and in-world presence are shared as part of multiplayer functionality.
  • Legal and safety recipients. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or a governmental request; enforce our Terms; investigate abuse or security issues; or protect the rights, safety, and property of users, cubacadabra, or others.
  • Business transfers. Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction, subject to applicable confidentiality and data-protection requirements.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising based on the current Service. We require third parties that receive information from us to provide protections consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Small pieces of storage, practical purposes.

The website may use essential browser storage and technologies needed to deliver pages, remember your player settings, maintain security, and provide the multiplayer experience. We do not currently use non-essential advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. Your browser settings may allow you to block or clear storage, although some Service features may not work as a result.

9. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed.

Active connection and in-world presence information is generally kept for the duration of a session and is removed when the connection ends, subject to short-lived technical, security, and backup processes. A player identifier and chosen name may be stored after a session so that the Service can remember the name, enforce name uniqueness, prevent abuse, and support continuity between sessions. We retain support, safety, and technical records for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the matter, protect the Service, comply with law, or resolve disputes.

Retention periods may vary based on the type and sensitivity of information, the purpose for which it was collected, security and fraud-prevention needs, and legal requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete it, anonymize it, or securely isolate it in accordance with our ordinary deletion practices.

10. Your choices and requests

You can stop, clear, or ask us to delete.

You may stop using the Service at any time. You can clear local browser storage or uninstall the mobile app to remove information stored on your device. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your player identifier, contact support@cubacadabra.com. Please provide your player name and, if available, your player identifier so that we can locate the correct record. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request and may retain information where required or permitted by law, or where needed for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

Deleting a persistent player record may release your chosen name, remove continuity between sessions, and prevent us from linking future activity to prior activity. We will handle requests within the time required by applicable law.

11. Regional privacy rights

Local law may provide additional choices.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know about, access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of certain personal information, or to appeal a decision about a privacy request. You may also have the right to withdraw consent or lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. Contact us at the address above to exercise an applicable right. We will not discriminate against you for making a lawful privacy request.

12. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13.

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information, please contact us at support@cubacadabra.com. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. Where local law sets a higher age or additional requirements, those rules apply.

13. Security

We use reasonable safeguards.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and destruction. No internet transmission, storage system, or service can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device and for not sharing information through a player name or support message that you want to keep private.

14. International processing

Information may travel across borders.

cubacadabra and its service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Those countries may have privacy laws different from your own. When required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers and take steps to protect information in accordance with this Policy and applicable law.

15. Third-party links and platforms

Other services have their own rules.

The Service may link to or depend on third-party websites, operating systems, app stores, or services. We do not control their privacy practices and are not responsible for them. Review the privacy policy of each third party before providing it with information. This Policy applies only to information handled by cubacadabra under our control.

16. Changes to this Policy

We will post updates here.

We may update this Policy when the Service changes, our practices change, or the law requires it. We will post the revised version on this page and update the effective date. If a change is material, we will provide additional notice when reasonably practicable. Your continued use of the Service after the updated Policy becomes effective means that you acknowledge the update.

17. Contact us

Privacy questions are welcome.

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, child-safety concerns, or other privacy notices, contact cubacadabra at support@cubacadabra.com. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.
