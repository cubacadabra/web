Terms of use

A small set of rules for a shared world.

By using Cubacadabra, you agree to help keep this little world welcoming, fair, and able to work for everyone in it.

Use the world well

Be a good neighbor.

Don’t harass other players, disrupt the service, impersonate someone else, or use Cubacadabra to break the law. Attempts to gain unauthorized access are not welcome here.

Your part

Keep your name and access yours.

You’re responsible for the player name and activity associated with your session. Choose a name that doesn’t identify or misrepresent another person.

A work in progress

Things will change.

Cubacadabra is experimental. The world, its features, and these terms may change as it grows. We may pause or limit access when maintenance, safety, or development requires it.

If something goes wrong

We may step in.

We may remove activity or restrict access when it harms the world or the people using it. If you stop agreeing to these terms, please stop using the service.
