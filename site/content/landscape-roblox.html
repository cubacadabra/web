Landscape / Platform profile

Roblox is the network-effect benchmark.

Roblox owns the broadest combination of players, creators, discovery, monetization, and cultural familiarity in this category. Any alternative has to respect that scale before it can explain its difference.

What Roblox gets right

The platform is a habit, not just an engine.

Roblox combines a huge audience with a mature creator funnel: make, publish, discover, play, and earn in one ecosystem. That loop is the thing an open runtime does not automatically create.

Its planned expansion toward experiences that can run as standalone apps makes the boundary between “platform” and “game distribution” more fluid. The moat is moving, but it is still a moat.
