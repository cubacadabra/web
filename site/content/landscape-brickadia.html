Brickadia owns the joy of bricks.

Brickadia is an Early Access, multiplayer brick-building sandbox with an editor, online co-op, and a growing Steam audience.

What it proves

A focused creative verb can be enough.

Brickadia is not trying to be every kind of user-generated platform. It is brick-building first, and that focus gives the experience a clear texture. Early Access traction and positive reviews make it a useful reminder that people will show up for a well-defined making loop.

cubacadabra’s equivalent should be a coherent world-building loop that feels good before the platform fills in every future promise.
