Landscape / Platform profile

Luanti is the mature open-source proof.

Formerly Minetest, Luanti is an open-source voxel game creation platform with Lua APIs, multiplayer, games, mods, and a long-running community ecosystem.

What it proves

Open ecosystems can compound over time.

Luanti has the strongest maturity story among the open-source examples in this set. Its center of gravity is deliberately voxel and Minecraft-shaped, but the underlying pattern is important: a permissive engine, a modding API, community content, and transparent development can keep creating surface area.

cubacadabra does not need to imitate the voxel model. It needs to make contribution and ownership just as legible in a platform shaped around shared worlds rather than blocks.
