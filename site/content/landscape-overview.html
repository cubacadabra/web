Landscape / Overview

Roblox Alternatives

Here’s the honest lay of the land. A Roblox developer looking around today will find real alternatives, but also meaningful gaps. cubacadabra is aiming for the space between “closed platform” and “interesting prototype.”

Candid as of September 2026

01 / The category

People want a place to make worlds.

That demand is already being served by large closed platforms, focused sandboxes, and a handful of open-source projects.

02 / The opening

Open does not have to mean unfinished.

The opportunity is a platform that is transparent about its boundaries while still feeling coherent, welcoming, and useful today.

03 / Our bet

Rust at the center. People at the edge.

A shared Rust runtime can travel across web and native clients. The hard work is earning creators, players, trust, and great worlds.

The position

The sweet spot is not “first.” It is open, respectful, and realistic.

s&box is technically serious. Polytoria makes a smaller classic model feel tangible. The Mirror is unusually close to the open-source thesis. Luanti proves that an open creator ecosystem can endure, even with a voxel-shaped center. Brickadia and Core each own a distinct creative lane.

That leaves a space nobody really owns yet: a cross-platform creator platform that says what is ready, what is experimental, and who gets to keep control.
